A little about me

About

I’m Nafisat, a Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Minna. My work brings together medical imaging, robotics, and intelligent systems.

I enjoy working across software and hardware: from adapting electronic medical records for clinical use to assembling a low-field MRI scanner and a robotic field-mapping system. Alongside research, I contribute to scientific publishing, student leadership, and community programmes.
